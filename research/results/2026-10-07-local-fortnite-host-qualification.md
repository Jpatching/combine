Fresh source reads corroborate two distinct initialization gaps. The [client branch](https://github.com/PongooDev/Core/blob/6dc0e9a0ff91d619b49b0d3772308ff41cb51cd0/Core/Core/Private/Client.cpp#L23-L80) initializes console/CheatManager behavior rather than loading a world. These facts explain why flags alone do not establish a self-starting local world; they do not explain the earlier process exit.

Whole-DLL loading is not selected. The retained audit identifies [unconditional player-validation replacement](https://github.com/PongooDev/Core/blob/6dc0e9a0ff91d619b49b0d3772308ff41cb51cd0/Core/FortniteGame/Private/FortGameSession.cpp#L4-L22), [challenge-response replacement](https://github.com/PongooDev/Core/blob/6dc0e9a0ff91d619b49b0d3772308ff41cb51cd0/Core/Engine/Source/Runtime/Engine/Private/NetConnection.cpp#L84-L104), and [MCP credential changes](https://github.com/PongooDev/Core/blob/6dc0e9a0ff91d619b49b0d3772308ff41cb51cd0/Core/FortniteGame/Private/Mcp/McpProfileGroup.cpp#L4-L14).
