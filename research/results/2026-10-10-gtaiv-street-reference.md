## Verified primary route evidence

[YuGiOhFm2002's original stunt-jump guide, #014–015](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps3/933036-grand-theft-auto-iv/faqs/52838)
gives a connected route: Sing Sing Avenue to Flanger Street, then west onto
Northern Expressway; pass a barrier at Leavenworth Avenue and a second barrier;
continue on the unfinished bridge to a leaning green metal ramp; land on the
next bridge section; continue over a second ramp to the west side of Rocket
Street. This is a named approach plus ordered geometry, substantially stronger
than a general claim that an unfinished bridge exists in Bohan.

[Ratchet12345's original walkthrough, stunt jumps #16–17](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ps3/933036-grand-theft-auto-iv/faqs/54410)
independently starts at Northern Expressway / Leavenworth Avenue near the Bohan
strip club, directs the player southwest through construction barriers, then
up the unfinished road to a ramp and the next platform. Its #17 continues to
another ramp but does **not** name Rocket Street as the landing. The two guides
use different numbering; compare their physical route, not their jump numbers.

There are nearby alternatives. YuGiOhFm2002 #016 describes another unfinished
overpass approached from Rocket Street / Attica Avenue, with a blue ramp and
Guantanamo Avenue landing; #017 describes a separate waterside ramp reached
through Jackhammer Street. Ratchet #14 and #15 likewise distinguish the
Attica/Rocket approach and a Lompoc/Worm waterside alley. These alternatives
make a generic industrial bridge/railway silhouette insufficient. The cited
authors describe observed gameplay; they are not publisher maps or authority
for resource identity.

[Psy's original guide](https://www.igrandtheftauto.com/gta4/guides/unique-jumps)
also separates the two-stage unfinished bridge jump in Industrial (#14–15),
the adjacent-to-train-bridge waterside jump (#16), and a South Bohan building-site
approach (#17). Its [author-created jump map](https://www.igrandtheftauto.com/gta4/maps/unique-jumps)
provides start/direction/landing annotations. The map page was verified, but
the full image was not visually inspected in this investigation; no map-derived
street or landmark match is claimed.
