TEST SYNERGY - Intervention input trial

Use this exact build. Report "accept this build" or describe what failed.
Other Synergy options are visible but are outside this trial's verified scope.
The inherited offline statistics error is recorded separately.

Actual Synergy controls (default bindings; the game's prompts reflect remaps):
Open: hold Aim + press Melee (DualSense L2+R3; keyboard right mouse + V).
Up / Down: Aim / Fire (L2 / R2; right / left mouse).
Select: Use (Square; E).
Back: Melee (R3; V). Back at the root closes the menu.
Sliders: Tactical / Frag (L1 / R1; G / F).
Release the opening chord before navigating. F6 and arrow navigation belong to
an earlier native menu and are not controls for this Synergy build.

1. Open Synergy. Use Weapon Options > Give Weapons > Sniper Rifles > Intervention.
   Confirm its original menu is readable and that Intervention becomes held.
2. Navigate with keyboard and controller. While open, fire/aim/use/jump/melee,
   movement, camera and J/both-stick skating controls must not affect gameplay.
3. Back to root and close while holding Fire. Nothing should fire until all
   controls return to neutral and you press Fire again. Then fire on foot.
4. Reopen. Change focus and return; disconnect/reconnect the controller. Release
   controls, then confirm the menu and normal play still recover.
5. Quit normally and relaunch with Test Synergy. Check 1280x720 and 1920x1080
   readability, and report any error, lost control, unexpected action or freeze.

The world keeps running while the menu is open. Testing is not acceptance;
acceptance, source publication, merge and release are recorded separately.
